# Project Overview

## Identity

- **Name:** HeatShield AI
- **Full title:** AI-Powered Hyperlocal Heat Impact Intelligence & Response Platform
- **Team:** ByteForce
- **Target demo:** 28 November 2026

## Problem context

City-wide weather alerts communicate broad conditions but may not show which local areas combine high heat exposure, social vulnerability and limited protective infrastructure. Weather, population, land-cover and service information may also be maintained separately.

The prototype explores how available indicators can be combined to support area-level prioritisation.

## Proposed solution

A map-led dashboard that:
- displays supported city areas;
- calculates a transparent relative assessment from documented indicators;
- explains contributing indicators;
- communicates data coverage and limitations; and
- suggests local response actions for human review.

## Intended users

Municipal and urban local-body teams; disaster-management and heat-action planning teams; public-health/community organisations; urban planners and researchers. These are intended users, not confirmed deployment partners.

## Assessment dimensions

1. **Heat exposure:** available weather and environmental signals relevant to heat.
2. **Social vulnerability:** available population/demographic indicators that may indicate greater need for protection.
3. **Infrastructure and services:** available indicators such as green/shaded spaces and access to relevant public services.

Exact indicators depend on source availability, geographic resolution, freshness and permitted use. Later data documentation will define the final set.

## Expected output

For each supported area: relative score/category; contributing indicators and direction; data coverage/quality notes; suggested response actions; and source/provenance information where available.

## Boundaries

This is a decision-support prototype. It does not replace official warnings, public-health advice, field assessment or municipal decision-making. It will not claim precise street-level temperatures, individual health outcomes, deaths, power outages or causal impact without validated data and methods.

## Demo success

A user can select a supported area, see an assessment, understand why it was produced, inspect limitations and view a relevant suggested action without relying on an unexplained score.
